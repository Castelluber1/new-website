Francophone Mobility Program

What it is

An International Mobility Program stream that lets Canadian employers outside Quebec hire French-speaking foreign workers without a Labour Market Impact Assessment. The work permit carries exemption code C16.

How it works

Your employer submits a job offer through the Employer Portal and pays the compliance fee. Because there is no LMIA, the file skips the labour market review. We prepare your work permit application and map the route to permanent residence.

Who can qualify

  • French at NCLC 5 or higher (speaking and listening)
  • Genuine job offer from a Canadian employer
  • Job located outside Quebec
  • No LMIA required from the employer
  • Admissible to Canada

Why the Francophone Mobility Program

For French speakers, this is one of the fastest and most flexible ways into the Canadian labour market.

No LMIA

The employer skips the Labour Market Impact Assessment, saving the government fee and weeks of processing.

Open to more jobs

The role does not have to be high-skilled, so more occupations qualify than under many other streams.

Employer-friendly

With no recruitment test to pass, employers are often more willing to hire through this route.

A bridge to PR

The French that qualifies you here also earns extra Express Entry points toward permanent residence.

Requirements and where applications stall

The program is broad, but these are the points that decide whether an application succeeds.

French language proof

You need French at NCLC 5 or higher in speaking and listening, proven with an approved test such as TEF Canada or TCF Canada. Booking the test early prevents most delays.

A genuine job offer

The offer must be real, outside Quebec, and submitted through the Employer Portal with the compliance fee paid. A weak or unclear offer is the most common problem.

Employer compliance

The employer must meet International Mobility Program obligations. We make sure the offer of employment is filed correctly so the work permit is not delayed.

Outside Quebec

The job must be located in a province or territory other than Quebec, which runs its own immigration programs.

Admissibility

Standard checks apply: police certificates, medical if required, and biometrics. Small inconsistencies across documents can create credibility issues.

Planning the PR path early

Treating the work permit as a stand-alone step is a missed opportunity. We plan the Express Entry route from day one so your Canadian experience counts toward PR.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Francophone Mobility Program open or closed in 2026?

The Francophone Mobility Program is open in 2026 and continues under the International Mobility Program. It supports French-speaking immigration to communities outside Quebec, which remains a federal priority.

What French level do you need?

You need French at NCLC 5 or higher in speaking and listening, proven with an approved test such as TEF Canada or TCF Canada. There is no English requirement for the program itself.

Does it require an LMIA?

No. The Francophone Mobility Program is LMIA-exempt under work permit code C16, so the employer does not have to complete a Labour Market Impact Assessment.

Does it lead to permanent residence?

It does not grant PR directly, but it leads to it. The Canadian work experience it provides, plus your French, positions you well for Express Entry, including French-language category draws.

Do you need a job offer?

Yes. You need a genuine job offer from a Canadian employer for a position outside Quebec. The employer submits the offer through the Employer Portal and pays the compliance fee before you apply.
