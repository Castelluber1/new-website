Nova Scotia Nominee Program

What it is

The NSNP lets the province of Nova Scotia nominate skilled workers, in-demand occupations, physicians, and entrepreneurs for permanent residence. A nomination is a powerful boost to your application.

How it works

Depending on the stream, you apply directly or are selected from the Express Entry pool. If nominated, you apply to IRCC for permanent residence. We plan both stages with you.

Who can qualify

  • Express Entry candidates in target occupations
  • Skilled workers with a Nova Scotia job offer
  • Physicians recruited by health authorities
  • Workers in in-demand occupations
  • Entrepreneurs and graduate entrepreneurs

NSNP streams

Nova Scotia runs several pathways. Choosing the right one for your profile is where a strong application starts.

Labour Market Priorities

Selects Express Entry candidates in occupations Nova Scotia targets, often without needing a job offer.

Nova Scotia Experience

Express Entry stream for candidates with recent Nova Scotia work experience.

Physician stream

For physicians recruited by the Nova Scotia Health Authority or the IWK Health Centre.

Entrepreneur streams

For experienced entrepreneurs and international graduate entrepreneurs who want to run a business in Nova Scotia.

NSNP requirements

Requirements differ by stream. These are the factors that decide most NSNP applications.

A targeted occupation

Labour Market Priorities draws focus on occupations Nova Scotia needs. Your occupation and NOC affect eligibility.

Express Entry profile

Several streams require an active federal Express Entry profile alongside meeting the stream criteria.

Language and education

An approved language test and, for foreign education, an Educational Credential Assessment are needed to meet stream minimums.

Work experience

Recent, relevant work experience is required, with specific rules for the Experience and Skilled Worker streams.

Job offer for some streams

The Skilled Worker and Physician streams need a Nova Scotia job offer; Labour Market Priorities may not.

A complete, consistent file

Small inconsistencies across your registration, forms, and proof documents are a common reason applications stall. We audit the file before submission.

Frequently asked questions

What is the NSNP?

The NSNP (Nova Scotia Nominee Program) is Nova Scotia's provincial nominee program. It nominates skilled workers, in-demand occupations, physicians, and entrepreneurs for Canadian permanent residence.

What are the NSNP streams?

The main streams include Labour Market Priorities (Express Entry-aligned), Nova Scotia Experience, Skilled Worker, the Physician stream, Occupations In-Demand, and Entrepreneur streams. Nova Scotia also joins the Atlantic Immigration Program.

How does the NSNP work with Express Entry?

Several NSNP streams draw from the federal Express Entry pool. A nomination adds 600 CRS points, which effectively guarantees an invitation to apply.

Do you need a job offer for the NSNP?

It depends on the stream. The Skilled Worker and Physician streams need a Nova Scotia job offer, while Labour Market Priorities can select Express Entry candidates without one.

How long does the NSNP take?

The NSNP has two stages: the provincial nomination, which varies by stream, then the federal PR application. The federal stage currently takes about 6 months as of September 2026.
