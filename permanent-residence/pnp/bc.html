British Columbia Provincial Nominee Program

What it is

The BC PNP lets the province of British Columbia nominate skilled workers, international graduates, and entrepreneurs for permanent residence. A BC nomination is a powerful boost to your Canadian immigration application.

How it works

You register in the Skills Immigration Registration System (SIRS), receive a score, and wait for an invitation to apply in a BC PNP draw. If nominated, you then apply to IRCC for permanent residence. We plan both stages with you.

Who can qualify

  • Skilled or semi-skilled workers with a BC job offer
  • International graduates from eligible programs
  • Tech workers in priority occupations
  • Health authority and childcare workers
  • Entrepreneurs investing in BC

BC PNP streams

British Columbia runs several pathways. Choosing the right one for your profile is where a strong application starts.

Skills Immigration

For skilled and semi-skilled workers with a BC job offer, including the Skilled Worker and Entry Level and Semi-Skilled categories.

International Graduate

For recent graduates of eligible Canadian institutions with a qualifying BC job offer, with a dedicated post-graduate option.

BC PNP Tech

A priority pathway for workers in in-demand technology occupations, with more frequent draws for eligible tech roles.

Entrepreneur Immigration

For experienced business owners and managers who want to invest in and actively manage a business in British Columbia.

BC PNP requirements and points

Your BC PNP score decides whether you get invited. These are the factors that matter most.

A qualifying job offer

Most Skills Immigration streams need an indeterminate, full-time job offer from a BC employer in an eligible occupation. The wage and occupation both affect your score.

Your SIRS score

BC ranks candidates on economic and human capital factors: experience, education, language, and the wage of your job offer. A higher score means an earlier invitation.

Language and education

An approved language test (English or French) and, in most cases, an Educational Credential Assessment for foreign education, are needed to claim full points.

Work experience

Directly related work experience raises your score. Semi-skilled roles qualify under specific categories with their own rules.

Choosing base vs Express Entry BC

Express Entry BC gives 600 CRS points if you are already in the federal pool. The base stream does not, but reaches candidates who are not Express Entry eligible.

A complete, consistent file

Small inconsistencies across your registration, forms, and proof documents are a common reason applications stall. We audit the file before submission.

Frequently asked questions

What is the BC PNP?

The BC PNP (British Columbia Provincial Nominee Program) lets British Columbia nominate skilled workers, graduates, and entrepreneurs for permanent residence. A provincial nomination adds significant weight to your immigration application.

What are the BC PNP streams?

The main streams are Skills Immigration (skilled and semi-skilled workers and international graduates) and Entrepreneur Immigration. Skills Immigration includes an Express Entry BC option that links to the federal pool.

How does the BC PNP work with Express Entry?

Express Entry BC is an enhanced nomination stream. If BC nominates you through it, you receive 600 additional CRS points in Express Entry, which effectively guarantees an invitation to apply.

Do you need a job offer for the BC PNP?

Most BC PNP Skills Immigration streams require a valid job offer from a British Columbia employer. Some international graduate and tech pathways have specific rules, so it depends on the stream.

How long does the BC PNP take?

BC PNP processing has two stages: the provincial nomination, which varies by stream, then the federal PR application. The federal stage currently takes about 6 months as of September 2026.
