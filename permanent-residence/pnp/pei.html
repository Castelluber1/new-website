Prince Edward Island PNP

What it is

The PEI PNP lets Prince Edward Island nominate skilled workers, critical workers, graduates, and entrepreneurs for permanent residence. A nomination is a powerful boost to your application.

How it works

You submit an Expression of Interest, and PEI selects candidates in regular draws. If nominated, you apply to IRCC for permanent residence. We plan both stages with you.

Who can qualify

  • Skilled workers with a PEI job offer
  • Critical workers in in-demand roles
  • Express Entry candidates with PEI ties
  • Graduates of eligible institutions
  • Entrepreneurs settling in PEI

PEI PNP streams

Prince Edward Island runs several pathways. Choosing the right one for your profile is where a strong application starts.

PEI Express Entry

For candidates in the federal Express Entry pool who show ties to Prince Edward Island.

Labour Impact: Skilled Worker

For skilled workers with a full-time job offer from a PEI employer.

Labour Impact: Critical Worker

For workers in specific in-demand roles PEI employers struggle to fill.

Business Work Permit

For experienced entrepreneurs who want to establish or buy and actively run a business in Prince Edward Island.

PEI PNP requirements

Requirements differ by stream. These are the factors that decide most PEI PNP applications.

A job offer for most streams

Labour Impact streams need a full-time job offer from a PEI employer in an eligible or critical occupation.

Ties to the province

The Express Entry stream favours candidates who can show a genuine connection to Prince Edward Island.

Language and education

An approved language test and, for foreign education, an Educational Credential Assessment are needed to meet stream minimums.

Work experience

Relevant work experience is required, with different rules for the Skilled and Critical Worker streams.

Intention to settle

You must show a genuine intention to live and work in Prince Edward Island as a permanent resident.

A complete, consistent file

Small inconsistencies across your registration, forms, and proof documents are a common reason applications stall. We audit the file before submission.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PEI PNP?

The PEI PNP (Prince Edward Island Provincial Nominee Program) nominates skilled workers, critical workers, graduates, and entrepreneurs who want to settle in Prince Edward Island for Canadian permanent residence.

What are the PEI PNP streams?

The main streams are Express Entry, the Labour Impact category (Skilled Worker, Critical Worker, and International Graduate), and the Business Work Permit stream. PEI also joins the Atlantic Immigration Program.

How does the PEI PNP work with Express Entry?

The PEI Express Entry stream selects candidates from the federal pool with ties to the province. A nomination adds 600 CRS points, which effectively guarantees an invitation to apply.

Do you need a job offer for the PEI PNP?

Most Labour Impact streams require a job offer from a PEI employer. The Critical Worker stream covers specific in-demand roles, and graduate and entrepreneur streams have their own rules.

How long does the PEI PNP take?

The PEI PNP has two stages: the provincial nomination, which varies by stream, then the federal PR application. The federal stage currently takes about 6 months as of September 2026.
