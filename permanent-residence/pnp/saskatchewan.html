Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program

What it is

The SINP lets the province of Saskatchewan nominate skilled workers, experienced workers already in the province, and entrepreneurs for permanent residence. A nomination is a powerful boost to your application.

How it works

You submit an Expression of Interest, receive a points score, and wait for an invitation in a SINP draw. If nominated, you apply to IRCC for permanent residence. We plan both stages with you.

Who can qualify

  • Skilled workers in in-demand occupations
  • Express Entry candidates with points
  • Workers already employed in Saskatchewan
  • Entrepreneurs starting a business
  • Farm owners and operators

SINP streams

Saskatchewan runs several pathways. Choosing the right one for your profile is where a strong application starts.

International Skilled Worker

Includes the Express Entry and Occupations In-Demand sub-categories, which can select candidates on points without a job offer.

Saskatchewan Experience

For workers already employed in Saskatchewan, including health, hospitality, and long-haul truck driver pathways.

Entrepreneur

For experienced business owners who want to invest in and operate a business in Saskatchewan.

Farm Owner and Operator

For experienced farmers with the capital to buy and operate a farm in Saskatchewan.

SINP requirements

Requirements differ by stream. These are the factors that decide most SINP applications.

An in-demand occupation

International Skilled Worker sub-categories favour occupations Saskatchewan needs. Your occupation and NOC matter for eligibility.

Your SINP points score

Saskatchewan ranks candidates on a points grid covering education, experience, language, age, and connection to the province.

Language and education

An approved language test and, for foreign education, an Educational Credential Assessment are needed to meet stream minimums.

Work experience

Relevant, recent work experience raises your score, with specific rules for the Experience and Skilled Worker streams.

Job offer for some streams

The Saskatchewan Experience stream requires you to already work in the province; Occupations In-Demand does not require a job offer.

A complete, consistent file

Small inconsistencies across your registration, forms, and proof documents are a common reason applications stall. We audit the file before submission.

Frequently asked questions

What is the SINP?

The SINP (Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program) is Saskatchewan's provincial nominee program. It nominates skilled workers, experienced workers already in the province, and entrepreneurs for Canadian permanent residence.

What are the SINP streams?

The main streams are International Skilled Worker (Express Entry and Occupations In-Demand sub-categories), Saskatchewan Experience (for workers already in the province), and Entrepreneur and Farm streams.

How does the SINP work with Express Entry?

The International Skilled Worker Express Entry sub-category selects candidates from the federal pool. A nomination adds 600 CRS points, which effectively guarantees an invitation to apply.

Do you need a job offer for the SINP?

It depends on the sub-category. Occupations In-Demand and Express Entry can select candidates without a job offer, while the Saskatchewan Experience stream requires you to already work in the province.

How long does the SINP take?

The SINP has two stages: the provincial nomination, which varies by stream, then the federal PR application. The federal stage currently takes about 6 months as of September 2026.
