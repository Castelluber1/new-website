Alberta Advantage Immigration Program

What it is

The AAIP lets the province of Alberta nominate workers, graduates, and entrepreneurs who want to live and work in Alberta for permanent residence. An Alberta nomination is a powerful boost to your application.

How it works

You apply to the stream that fits your situation, Alberta assesses your eligibility and ties to the province, and if nominated you apply to IRCC for permanent residence. We plan both stages with you.

Who can qualify

  • Workers in Alberta on a valid work permit
  • Express Entry candidates with Alberta ties
  • Workers settling in rural Alberta communities
  • Tourism and hospitality workers
  • Entrepreneurs and graduate entrepreneurs

AAIP streams

Alberta runs several pathways. Choosing the right one for your situation is where a strong application starts.

Alberta Opportunity Stream

For workers already in Alberta on a valid work permit, with a job offer from an Alberta employer in an eligible occupation.

Alberta Express Entry Stream

For candidates in the federal Express Entry pool who have ties to Alberta. A nomination adds 600 CRS points.

Rural Renewal Stream

For workers with a job offer in a designated rural Alberta community that supports their application.

Entrepreneur streams

For graduate entrepreneurs, rural entrepreneurs, and farm investors who want to build or buy a business in Alberta.

AAIP requirements

Requirements differ by stream. These are the factors that decide most AAIP applications.

Ties to Alberta

Most streams expect a genuine connection to Alberta, such as current work in the province, a job offer, or graduation from an Alberta institution.

A qualifying job offer

The Alberta Opportunity Stream needs a full-time job offer from your current Alberta employer in an eligible occupation, with the required wage.

Language and education

An approved language test and, in most cases, an Educational Credential Assessment for foreign education are needed to meet stream minimums.

Work experience

Directly related work experience is required, with the amount and type depending on the stream you apply under.

Express Entry ties

For the Alberta Express Entry Stream, you must already be in the federal pool and show your intention to live and work in Alberta.

A complete, consistent file

Small inconsistencies across your registration, forms, and proof documents are a common reason applications stall. We audit the file before submission.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Alberta PNP (AAIP)?

The AAIP (Alberta Advantage Immigration Program) is Alberta's provincial nominee program. It nominates workers, graduates, and entrepreneurs who want to settle in Alberta for Canadian permanent residence.

What are the AAIP streams?

The main streams are the Alberta Opportunity Stream (for workers already in Alberta on a valid work permit), the Alberta Express Entry Stream, the Rural Renewal Stream, the Tourism and Hospitality Stream, and entrepreneur streams.

How does the Alberta Express Entry Stream work?

It lets Alberta select candidates from the federal Express Entry pool who have ties to Alberta. A nomination adds 600 CRS points, which effectively guarantees an invitation to apply for permanent residence.

Do you need a job offer for the AAIP?

It depends on the stream. The Alberta Opportunity Stream requires you to already be working in Alberta on a valid work permit. Some Express Entry and rural streams have different job-offer rules.

How long does the AAIP take?

The AAIP has two stages: the provincial nomination, which varies by stream, then the federal PR application. The federal stage currently takes about 6 months as of September 2026.
