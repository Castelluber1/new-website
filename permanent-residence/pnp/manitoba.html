Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program

What it is

The MPNP lets the province of Manitoba nominate workers, graduates, and business investors with a genuine connection to Manitoba for permanent residence. A nomination is a powerful boost to your application.

How it works

You submit an Expression of Interest, receive a score based on your Manitoba connection and profile, and wait for an invitation in an EOI draw. If nominated, you apply to IRCC for PR. We plan both stages with you.

Who can qualify

  • Workers already employed in Manitoba
  • Skilled workers overseas with Manitoba ties
  • Graduates of Manitoba institutions
  • Candidates with close family in Manitoba
  • Business investors settling in Manitoba

MPNP streams

Manitoba runs several pathways. Choosing the right one for your profile is where a strong application starts.

Skilled Worker in Manitoba

For workers already employed in Manitoba with a full-time, long-term job offer from their current employer.

Skilled Worker Overseas

For skilled workers abroad with an established connection to Manitoba through family, past study or work, or a draw.

International Education

For graduates of Manitoba institutions, including career employment and graduate internship pathways.

Business Investor Stream

For experienced business owners and managers who want to invest in and operate a business in Manitoba.

MPNP requirements

Requirements differ by stream. These are the factors that decide most MPNP applications.

A connection to Manitoba

Most streams require a genuine connection: current work, close family, past study or work, or an invitation through a draw.

Your EOI score

Manitoba ranks candidates by an Expression of Interest score based on age, language, education, experience, and Manitoba connection.

Language and education

An approved language test and, for foreign education, an Educational Credential Assessment are needed to meet stream minimums.

Work experience

Relevant work experience raises your score, with different rules for the in-Manitoba and overseas streams.

Settlement intention

You must show a genuine intention to live, work, and settle in Manitoba as a permanent resident.

A complete, consistent file

Small inconsistencies across your registration, forms, and proof documents are a common reason applications stall. We audit the file before submission.

Frequently asked questions

What is the MPNP?

The MPNP (Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program) is Manitoba's provincial nominee program. It nominates workers, international graduates, and business investors with ties to Manitoba for Canadian permanent residence.

What are the MPNP streams?

The main streams are Skilled Worker in Manitoba (for workers already employed in the province), Skilled Worker Overseas (with a Manitoba connection), International Education, and the Business Investor Stream.

How does the MPNP work with Express Entry?

Manitoba has an Express Entry pathway within Skilled Worker streams. A nomination adds 600 CRS points in Express Entry, which effectively guarantees an invitation to apply for permanent residence.

Do you need a connection to Manitoba?

Most MPNP streams require a genuine connection to Manitoba, such as current employment, a job offer, close family, past study or work, or an invitation through a recruitment mission.

How long does the MPNP take?

The MPNP has two stages: the provincial nomination, which varies by stream, then the federal PR application. The federal stage currently takes about 6 months as of September 2026.
