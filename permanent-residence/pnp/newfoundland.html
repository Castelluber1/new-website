Newfoundland and Labrador PNP

What it is

The NLPNP lets Newfoundland and Labrador nominate skilled workers, graduates, and entrepreneurs with a connection to the province for permanent residence. A nomination is a powerful boost to your application.

How it works

Depending on the stream, you apply directly or are selected from the Express Entry pool with a provincial job offer. If nominated, you apply to IRCC for PR. We plan both stages with you.

Who can qualify

  • Skilled workers with an NL job offer
  • Express Entry candidates with an NL offer
  • Graduates of eligible institutions
  • Workers with priority in-demand skills
  • Entrepreneurs and graduate entrepreneurs

NLPNP streams

Newfoundland and Labrador runs several pathways. Choosing the right one for your profile is where a strong application starts.

Express Entry Skilled Worker

For candidates in the federal Express Entry pool with a job offer from a Newfoundland and Labrador employer.

Skilled Worker

For skilled workers with a job offer from a provincial employer, outside Express Entry.

Priority Skills

For candidates with high-demand skills the province is actively recruiting, including a tech-focused path.

Entrepreneur streams

For international entrepreneurs and graduate entrepreneurs who want to run a business in the province.

NLPNP requirements

Requirements differ by stream. These are the factors that decide most NLPNP applications.

A job offer or in-demand skill

Most streams need a job offer from a provincial employer; the Priority Skills stream targets specific high-demand skills.

Express Entry profile

The Express Entry Skilled Worker stream requires an active federal Express Entry profile alongside your job offer.

Language and education

An approved language test and, for foreign education, an Educational Credential Assessment are needed to meet stream minimums.

Work experience

Relevant work experience is required, with the amount depending on the stream you apply under.

Intention to settle

You must show a genuine intention to live and work in Newfoundland and Labrador as a permanent resident.

A complete, consistent file

Small inconsistencies across your registration, forms, and proof documents are a common reason applications stall. We audit the file before submission.

Frequently asked questions

What is the NLPNP?

The NLPNP (Newfoundland and Labrador Provincial Nominee Program) nominates skilled workers, graduates, and entrepreneurs with a connection to the province for Canadian permanent residence.

What are the NLPNP streams?

The main streams are Express Entry Skilled Worker, Skilled Worker, International Graduate, Priority Skills, and entrepreneur streams. The province also joins the Atlantic Immigration Program.

How does the NLPNP work with Express Entry?

The Express Entry Skilled Worker stream selects candidates from the federal pool with a provincial job offer. A nomination adds 600 CRS points, which effectively guarantees an invitation to apply.

Do you need a job offer for the NLPNP?

Most streams need a job offer from a Newfoundland and Labrador employer. The Priority Skills stream targets specific in-demand skills, and graduate streams have their own rules.

How long does the NLPNP take?

The NLPNP has two stages: the provincial nomination, which varies by stream, then the federal PR application. The federal stage currently takes about 6 months as of September 2026.
