Chapter 02

Becoming a regulated consultant

In Canada, only a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC) or a licensed lawyer can legally charge for immigration advice. It is a regulated profession for a reason: the field is full of unlicensed "agents" and "facilitators" who take money and disappear when a file gets hard.

Larissa studied immigration laws, policies and procedures at the University of British Columbia and became licensed by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants, registration R710678. Her licence is Class L2 RCIC, active and in good standing, a status anyone can confirm on the public register in under a minute.

Chapter 03

Founding Up Immigration

Up Immigration Consulting grew out of a simple frustration: the Canadian immigration process is confusing, high-stakes, and easy to get wrong, and too many people navigate it alone or trust the wrong hands. Larissa built a family-run consultancy to make it clear and reliable, combining licensed immigration expertise with the HR and global-mobility experience that shaped her.

Today her work spans the pathways that matter most to the people she serves: Express Entry, Provincial Nominee Programs (with a focus on BC PNP), LMIA, and citizenship applications. She serves clients across Canada, with a particular closeness to the Brazilian community making the same journey she understands from the inside.

The goal was never to process files. It was to make sure the right people don't lose their shot on an avoidable mistake.
