What is Family Sponsorship?

Family sponsorship lets Canadian citizens and permanent residents bring certain family members to Canada as permanent residents. The most common stream is spousal and partner sponsorship, but Canada also has pathways for parents and grandparents and, in specific cases, other relatives.

Each stream has its own eligibility rules, documentation standards, and processing realities. The common thread is that the relationship must be genuine and thoroughly documented.

Sponsorship Streams

  • Spouse or common-law partner — keeping couples together in Canada.
  • Parents and grandparents — see PGP sponsorship processing times, or the Super Visa for extended visits without PR.
  • Other family situations — read our complete family sponsorship guide.
