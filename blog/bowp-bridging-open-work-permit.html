BOWP: The Bridging Open Work Permit Explained

6 min read•Oct 02, 2025

The Bridging Open Work Permit (BOWP) lets you keep working in Canada while your permanent residence application is being processed, so you don't have to stop working just because your current work permit is close to expiring.

What is a Bridging Open Work Permit?

A BOWP is a type of open work permit designed for people who have already applied for permanent residence and are waiting for a decision. "Open" means it isn't tied to a single employer, so you can generally keep working for most Canadian employers while your PR application moves through the system. It exists to bridge the gap between your temporary status and your permanent residence, hence the name.

Who is it for?

The BOWP is aimed at applicants who are already inside Canada on a valid work permit and have a permanent residence application in progress under an eligible economic class, such as Express Entry streams or certain provincial nominee pathways. If your current work authorization is running out before your PR is finalized, the BOWP is often the tool that keeps you legally working in the meantime.

Basic requirements

  • An eligible permanent residence application already in progress.
  • Valid status and residence in Canada at the time you apply.
  • A current or recently held work permit that makes you eligible to bridge.

Why it matters

Losing work authorization while waiting for PR can mean losing your job, your income, and sometimes your status. The BOWP removes that pressure by letting you continue working during the wait. Because eligibility depends on the specific PR stream you applied under and your current status, it's worth confirming your situation with a licensed consultant before your permit expires — the timing of when you apply matters.
