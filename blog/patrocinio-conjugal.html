How Spousal Sponsorship Works in Canada

5 min read • Oct 02, 2025

If you are the spouse or partner of a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, you may be eligible for spousal sponsorship — a family-class pathway to permanent residence in Canada.

Who can apply

Spousal sponsorship is built around a genuine relationship. In general, the following can qualify:

  • Legally married spouses, common-law partners, or conjugal partners.
  • Relationships that are authentic and genuine, supported by consistent evidence.
  • Sponsors and applicants without serious criminal issues or immigration inadmissibilities.

Documents and proof of the relationship

The heart of a spousal application is proving the relationship is real. Strong applications usually include:

  • A marriage certificate or proof of a common-law relationship.
  • A history of the relationship: shared living arrangements, photos, trips, messages, and joint financial records.
  • Reference letters from family and friends who know the couple.

Quick Tip

Organize your evidence by category (time together, finances, family, travel) with captions. This makes the file easier to assess and reduces requests for additional evidence.

Inland vs. Outland

You can apply from inside Canada (inland) or from outside Canada (outland). The choice can affect whether you can get an open work permit while you wait, as well as processing and appeal options. Because the right path depends on your situation, it's worth confirming with a licensed consultant which option fits you best.

Common mistakes

  • Inconsistencies between the forms and the supporting evidence.
  • Generic support letters with no specific detail.
  • Underestimating changes in status (address, employment, children) during processing.

Tempo de processamento

Variável conforme país e volume do IRCC. Recomendamos planejamento e organização prévia para reduzir retrabalhos.
