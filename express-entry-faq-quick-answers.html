CLB 7 is roughly IELTS 6.0 in each ability (or the CELPIP/TEF/TCF equivalent). There's no limit on retakes and only your best valid result counts — most people close a small gap by drilling the weakest ability and re-sitting. Your result must still be valid the day you submit.

Paid, full-time (or part-time equivalent) work in a TEER 0–3 occupation under NOC 2021. TEER 4 and 5 don't count. What matters is the duties you actually did matching the NOC — not the job title. Unpaid/volunteer work doesn't count.

Paid apprenticeship hours in a skilled trade (TEER 2 or 3) can count, as long as the work was paid and the duties match the NOC. The in-school portion generally doesn't count — only the paid on-the-job work. Keep pay stubs and a duties reference letter.

A refusal isn't final. You can reapply anytime, but only if you address the exact reason — reapplying with the same file usually gets the same result. Request the GCMS notes to see the officer's real concern, then submit a stronger application that answers it directly.

PR card renewal times change often and are published by IRCC — check the official processing-times tool. Apply well before expiry. An expired PR card doesn't mean you lost status; it only affects travelling back to Canada.

A visitor record extends your authorized stay inside Canada and has its own IRCC processing time that changes over time. If you apply online before your current status expires, you generally keep maintained (implied) status while you wait.

Parent/grandparent sponsorship (PGP) timelines are long and vary — check IRCC's current times. Access each year depends on IRCC's invitation-based intake, so being invited is the first step. The Super Visa is often faster for parents who want to visit for long periods.

For Express Entry, education done outside Canada generally needs an ECA from a designated organization (WES, IQAS, ICES, CES) to earn CRS points. A Canadian credential doesn't need one. The ECA confirms your foreign degree equals a Canadian one.
