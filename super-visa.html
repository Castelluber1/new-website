What is the Super Visa?

The Super Visa is a multi-entry temporary resident visa for the parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and permanent residents. Unlike a standard visitor visa, which generally allows shorter stays, the Super Visa is designed for extended visits and can remain valid for several years, letting families spend long, uninterrupted time together in Canada without committing to permanent immigration.

It has specific requirements around the inviting family member, financial support, and medical insurance. We help you confirm eligibility and assemble an application that holds up to an officer's review.
