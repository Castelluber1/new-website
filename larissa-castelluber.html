How to verify Larissa's licence with CICC

Only a licensed RCIC or a Canadian lawyer can legally charge for immigration advice. You should always be able to verify any consultant independently. Here is exactly how to confirm Larissa's licence on the official public register:

  1. Go to the CICC public register: college-ic.ca/find-a-consultant, then open "Search for an RCIC".
  2. In First Name Contains, type Larissa.
  3. In Last Name Contains, type Castelluber.
  4. Click Find. The result shows College ID R710678, company UP Immigration Consulting, and Entitled to Practise: Yes.
  5. Click Select to open the full profile, or go directly to her public CICC profile.
