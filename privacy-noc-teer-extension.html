Privacy Policy — NOC & TEER Finder

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the NOC & TEER Finder for Canada Express Entry Chrome extension, published by Up Immigration Consulting ("we", "us", "our").

Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or user data whatsoever.

How the extension works

The extension contains a fixed, built-in list of professions with their NOC 2021 codes and TEER levels. When you type a profession, the matching is done entirely on your own device, inside the extension popup. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. There is no server, no account, no login, and no tracking.

  • No personal information is requested or collected.
  • No browsing activity, clicks, or keystrokes are logged.
  • No cookies or local storage are used to persist your input.
  • No data is transmitted to us or to any third party.

Permissions

The extension requests no special browser permissions. It cannot read your tabs, your browsing history, or the content of any website.

Third-party links

The extension popup contains links to the official Government of Canada Job Bank / NOC tool and to our own website. Following those links takes you to external sites governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to this policy

If the extension ever changes in a way that affects data handling, we will update this page and revise the "last updated" date above.
