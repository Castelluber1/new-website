2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect on the Site includes:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Consultation Data: Immigration background, visa status, family composition, educational history (when provided for assessment)
  • Communication: Messages you send via contact forms, email, or WhatsApp

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain immigration consulting services
  • Respond to your inquiries and communicate about your consultation
  • Process payments and send invoices
  • Send transactional emails (appointment confirmations, document requests)
  • Improve our website and services based on usage analytics

  • Prevent fraud and enhance security

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our Services and comply with applicable laws. Generally:
